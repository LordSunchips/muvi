## What this app is

muvi is a personal movie-ranking app. You add films you've watched, rank them against each
other, and the app keeps your ordered list.

## What we collect

We collect only what's needed to run your account:

- **Email address** — used as your login identifier. Nothing else; we don't send marketing.
- **Password** — stored only as a bcrypt hash. We never store or have access to the plain text.
- **Your movie library** — the films you add, their rankings, buckets, scores, watch dates, and
  any notes you write.
- **Your display preference** — how you'd like scores calculated.

We do **not** collect analytics, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or device
identifiers. There is no third-party tracking SDK in the app.

## How your data is used

Solely to provide the app: to authenticate you and to store and display your library. We do not
sell your data, share it with third parties for their own purposes, or use it for advertising.

## Where your data lives

Your account data is stored on servers operated by our hosting providers, Render and Turso, in
the United States. Access is restricted to the app's maintainer.

## Third-party services

Movie titles, artwork, and metadata come from **The Movie Database (TMDB)**. When you search for
a film, your search text is sent to TMDB to return results. Movie posters are loaded directly
from TMDB's servers to your device, which means TMDB receives your device's IP address as part
of that request. TMDB's privacy policy: https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy

This product uses the TMDb API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDb.

## Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account from inside the app: **Settings → Delete account**. This
erases your account, your library, your rankings, and your notes. It is immediate and cannot be
undone.

## Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, it is
removed.

## Children

muvi is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
